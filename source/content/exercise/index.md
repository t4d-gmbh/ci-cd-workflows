## LaTeX Article CI/CD Pipeline
{% else %}
**LaTeX Article CI/CD Pipeline**

{% endif %}

In this exercise, you will work on a LaTeX article that includes a bibliography. 
You will learn how to set up an automated CI/CD pipeline to compile the LaTeX document and publish it as a PDF file.
This setup will ensure that your document is consistently compiled with all the necessary dependencies and configurations.


_Think of it as Overleaf, powered by <i class="fab fa-gitlab"></i>/<i class="fab fa-github"></i>_ 😉.

By the end of this exercise, you will have a practical automation workflow that boosts your productivity and maintains the quality of your LaTeX documents.

We start with <i class="fab fa-gitlab"></i> Pipelines, so head over to the <i class="fab fa-gitlab"></i> [Latex Article](https://gitlab.com/t4d-gmbh/teaching/gitws/latex-article) Project and follow the instructions in the `README.md`.


**Homework:**

As homework, go to the <i class="fab fa-github"></i> [LaTeX Article Template](https://github.com/matteodelucchi/latex-article-template) Repository and follow the instructions in the `README.md`.
